## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does junk removal cost in Oahu in 2026?**

Most companies charge by volume. Expect roughly $150-$400 for a small load (a few items or a fraction of a truck) and $400-$800+ for a full truck load. Hawaii disposal fees are higher than the mainland due to limited landfill capacity and shipping costs for recycled materials. Always get a free on-site estimate before committing.

**Is there free junk removal in Oahu?**

Yes. The City and County of Honolulu offers free bulky item pickup for residents. You can schedule up to 6 items per pickup through the city's website or by calling 768-3200. The tradeoff is limited scheduling windows, a 6-item cap, and no labor assistance. Items must be at the curb.

**Should I use a local company or a national franchise?**

Local companies typically offer more flexible pricing, understand Hawaii's disposal regulations firsthand, and keep revenue in the community. National franchises offer online booking, brand consistency, and sometimes same-day availability. For most residential jobs on Oahu, a local operator gives you better value. For urgent same-day needs, a franchise may have more scheduling flexibility.

**What items can junk removal companies in Oahu not take?**

No junk removal company in Hawaii can legally haul hazardous waste: paint, solvents, pesticides, asbestos, propane tanks, motor oil, medical waste, or biohazard materials. These require the City and County of Honolulu's [Household Hazardous Waste program](https://www.honolulu.gov/opala) or a licensed hazardous waste handler.
