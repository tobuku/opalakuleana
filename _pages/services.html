Our Services

From single-item pickups to whole-property cleanouts, ʻŌpala Kuleana handles it all.

Furniture Removal

Sofas, sectionals, recliners, dining sets, dressers, bed frames we remove and haul away any type of furniture, no matter the size. We do the heavy lifting so you don't have to.

Appliance Removal

Refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, stoves, water heaters we handle proper disposal for all major appliances, including items that require special handling under Hawaii regulations.

E-Waste & Electronics

Old televisions, computers, laptops, monitors, printers, cables, and small electronics. Hawaii has strict e-waste regulations we handle disposal responsibly to keep toxic materials out of our island's landfills.

Construction Debris

Lumber, drywall, tile, concrete chunks, scrap metal, old flooring, insulation post-renovation and demolition cleanup hauled away fast so you can move on to the next phase of your project.

Estate Cleanouts

Full-property clearing for estates, inherited homes, or properties being prepared for sale or rental. We work respectfully and efficiently to clear all items, leaving the space ready for its next chapter.

Yard Waste

Tree trimmings, branches, stumps, palm fronds, green debris, soil, and gravel. Hawaii's tropical growth means yard waste accumulates fast we haul it away before it becomes a problem.

Hot Tub Removal

Old or broken hot tubs and spas are heavy, bulky, and awkward to remove. Our crew handles safe disconnection and full removal, leaving your yard clean and clear when we’re done.

Commercial Cleanouts

Office furniture, retail fixtures, warehouse inventory, break room equipment we handle commercial space cleanouts for businesses throughout Oahu. Schedule around your operating hours.

Mattress Removal

Old mattresses, box springs, and bed frames picked up and disposed of responsibly. Mattresses can be difficult to dispose of on your own we make it simple.

Storage Unit Cleanouts

Ready to close out a storage unit? We’ll clear everything out in one trip so you can stop paying monthly rent on space you no longer need.

Important to Know

What We Cannot Remove

For safety and legal reasons, certain materials require specialized disposal that we’re not equipped to handle.

Prohibited Items

We are unable to haul hazardous or regulated materials. If you have items on this list, please contact the City & County of Honolulu’s Refuse Division or a licensed hazardous waste handler.

  • Paint & Solvents
  • Pesticides & Herbicides
  • Asbestos-containing materials
  • Propane & fuel tanks
  • Motor oil & automotive fluids
  • Medical waste
  • Biological / biohazard materials
  • Explosives or ammunition
  • Flammable liquids
  • Uncontained chemicals

Not sure if your item qualifies? Contact us and we’ll let you know before you book.
