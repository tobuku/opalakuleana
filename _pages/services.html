What We Remove

Full-Service Junk Removal

Residential or commercial, one item or a full property we haul it all across Oahu. Free estimates, transparent pricing.

Our Services

From single-item pickups to whole-property cleanouts, ʻŌpala Kuleana handles it all.

Furniture Removal

Mattresses and refrigerators are the top two most common calls we get, with stoves, tubs, and TVs rounding out the top 10. Sometimes people have their entire living room set replaced and we dump the old before they receive the new - or sometimes on the same day. Big or small, we do the heavy lifting so you don’t have to.

Appliance Removal

Fridges, washers, dryers, stoves, water heaters - we handle them all. If you can get your appliance outside, that really helps both you and us as we’d like to minimize the likelihood of your old appliance scraping or scratching your house during removal. We prefer not to enter homes, but if you’re unable to move the item on your own, we understand and do our best to explain the risks involved.

E-Waste & Electronics

Old televisions, computers, laptops, monitors, printers, cables, and small electronics. We normally see e-waste as part of a business or rent-ready clean out where the previous owner included electronics in the items to haul away. Hawaii has strict e-waste regulations and we handle disposal responsibly.

Construction Debris

Lumber, drywall, tile, concrete chunks, scrap metal, old flooring, insulation - we’re able to remove construction debris before, during, and after any renovations you or your contractor may be working on. Hauled away so you can move on to the next phase of your project.

Estate Cleanouts

Our process is for you to identify and introduce us to the items you’d like removed. We provide you an estimate, and if you agree we accept your signature and payment, then remove everything you’ve pointed out. We work with families, property managers, and realtors - leaving the space ready for its next chapter.

Yard Waste

If you have cut trees, bushes, or green waste you’d like hauled away, we can take it. We are unable to cut trees or bushes, but we are able to remove any debris you may have after cutting. Palm fronds, branches, stumps, green debris, soil, and gravel - Hawaii’s tropical growth means it accumulates fast.

Hot Tub Removal

We have hauled and dumped jacuzzis before. As long as it’s empty, we can haul and dump it for you. Our crew handles safe removal, leaving your yard clean and clear when we’re done.

Commercial Cleanouts

We primarily get calls from property managers who need old office furniture and equipment removed and dumped. Most of the time the PM is trying to get the property rent-ready for the next business tenant. A slightly different process for the PM but the same execution for us. We schedule around your operating hours.

Mattress Removal

We pick up, haul, and dump mattresses. We prefer to have them recycled, but understandably, some mattresses are better off dumped. Old mattresses, box springs, and bed frames picked up and disposed of responsibly.

Storage Unit Cleanouts

We’ve received calls from people who haven’t gotten around to removing one piece of clutter that turned into a pile of clutter. Ready to close out a storage unit? We’ll clear everything out so you can stop paying monthly rent on space you no longer need.

Important to Know

What We Cannot Remove

For safety and legal reasons, certain materials require specialized disposal that we’re not equipped to handle.

Prohibited Items

We are unable to haul hazardous or regulated materials. If you have items on this list, please contact the City & County of Honolulu’s Refuse Division or a licensed hazardous waste handler.

  • Paint & Solvents
  • Pesticides & Herbicides
  • Asbestos-containing materials
  • Propane & fuel tanks
  • Motor oil & automotive fluids
  • Medical waste
  • Biological / biohazard materials
  • Explosives or ammunition
  • Flammable liquids
  • Uncontained chemicals

Not sure if your item qualifies? Contact us and we’ll let you know before you book.
