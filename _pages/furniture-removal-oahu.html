Getting Rid of Old Furniture on Oahu Is Harder Than It Should Be

On the mainland, you can set a couch at the curb and it disappears by morning. On Oahu, it does not work that way. The City and County of Honolulu offers a bulky item pickup program, but scheduling can mean waiting a week or more — and some items require special handling. Renting a truck, loading furniture yourself, and driving to Waimanalo Gulch Sanitary Landfill adds time, fuel, and landfill fees that often rival the cost of just hiring someone.

Opala Kuleana picks up unwanted furniture across Oahu, handles loading, and takes care of responsible disposal — whether that means a donation drop-off at Habitat for Humanity ReStore Honolulu, diversion to the H-POWER waste-to-energy plant, or proper disposal at the landfill.

What We Remove

We pick up all types of household furniture, including:

  • Couches and sofas
  • Sectionals
  • Recliners
  • Dining tables and chairs
  • Entertainment centers
  • Bookshelves
  • Desks
  • Dressers and armoires
  • Bed frames and headboards

If you have a mattress or box spring to go along with the bed frame, we take that too. Mixed loads — furniture plus old appliances or general junk — are handled in a single trip.

A Typical Job

A Kailua homeowner replacing their living room set needed the old sectional, two recliners, and a coffee table removed before the new furniture arrived the next morning. They called us the afternoon before, we confirmed availability, and showed up at 8 AM the following day. The living room was cleared and ready before the delivery truck pulled up. The sectional was in decent shape and went to a donation partner; the recliners went to H-POWER.

Jobs like this are common across the island. We do similar work in Manoa, Kapolei, Aiea, Pearl City, Ewa Beach, and anywhere else on Oahu where furniture needs to move out and there is no easy DIY option.

Pricing

Furniture removal is priced by volume — specifically, how much space your items occupy in our truck. You are not paying by the piece; you are paying for the space used.

  • Single item (one couch, one recliner, one dresser): typically $75–$150
  • Living room set (sectional, coffee table, entertainment center): typically $200–$350
  • Full home or estate: contact us for a quote — see our estate cleanout page

We give you a firm quote before any work starts. If a staircase carry or difficult access is involved, we factor that in upfront. No surprise charges at the end.

When This Is Not the Right Option

If you have a single small item in good condition — a side table, a desk chair, one nightstand — the City and County of Honolulu's bulky item pickup program may be cheaper or free depending on your service area. You can also list small furniture on Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist and often get someone to haul it away at no cost.

Where we make the most sense: you have more than one item, you need it gone on a specific timeline, you do not want to coordinate strangers coming to your home, or the items are in poor condition and no one will take them for free.

Donation and Diversion

We try to keep usable furniture out of the landfill. Items that are structurally sound and free of major damage or odor are offered to organizations including:

  • Habitat for Humanity ReStore Honolulu
  • Salvation Army Hawaii

Items that cannot be donated go to the H-POWER waste-to-energy facility when eligible, or to Waimanalo Gulch Sanitary Landfill. We sort and route your items responsibly — you do not need to coordinate any of this yourself.
