Renovation Debris Adds Up Fast

A kitchen remodel generates old cabinets, countertops, flooring, drywall, plumbing fixtures, and appliances. A bathroom gut job produces tile, vanities, old tubs, and pipe. A full home renovation can fill multiple truckloads of debris before the new work even begins.

On Oahu, space on job sites is often limited. Homes in older neighborhoods like Kaimuki, Manoa, Kalihi, and Pearl City sit on tight lots with narrow driveways and shared access. Debris cannot sit in the yard or driveway for weeks without creating problems for the homeowner and the neighbors.

How We Support Renovation Projects

We work with contractors, general contractors, and homeowners running their own renovation projects. The process is straightforward: we come to the job site, assess the volume and type of debris, and give you a quote. Once approved, we load everything and haul it away.

For multi-week renovations, we can set up a recurring pickup schedule — weekly, biweekly, or as needed — so debris gets removed as the project progresses. This keeps the job site clean, keeps the crew productive, and prevents the kind of pile-up that slows everything down.

We handle mixed loads. Drywall, lumber, tile, old cabinets, fixtures, carpet, appliances, and general construction waste can all go in one trip. We sort what we can for recycling — metals, clean wood, and cardboard — and dispose of the rest at approved facilities.

Common Renovation Cleanout Jobs

  • Full kitchen gut — cabinets, countertops, flooring, appliances, and fixtures
  • Bathroom remodel — tile, vanity, tub or shower, plumbing
  • Whole-house renovation — everything from demolition phase through final cleanup
  • Flooring replacement — old carpet, tile, or hardwood removed and hauled
  • Roof or siding debris — shingles, underlayment, old siding panels
