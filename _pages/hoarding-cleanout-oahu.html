A Difficult Situation Handled With Care

Hoarding cleanouts are among the most challenging jobs in junk removal — not because of the physical labor, but because of the emotional weight involved. Families dealing with a loved one's hoarding situation often feel overwhelmed by the sheer volume of accumulated items and unsure where to start.

We have handled hoarding cleanouts across Oahu and understand that this is not a job you rush through. Important items — legal documents, family photos, jewelry, financial records — can be buried under layers of clutter. Our approach prioritizes careful sorting alongside efficient removal.

How We Work Through a Hoarding Cleanout

We start with a private walkthrough of the property with the family or property owner. We assess the volume, discuss what categories of items should be set aside for review, and outline a plan for the work. Then we give you a quote for the full job.

During the cleanout, our crew works room by room. Items identified for keeping are set aside in a clean area. Usable goods in decent condition are offered to donation partners. Everything else is loaded, hauled, and disposed of at approved facilities. We sort as we go so the family does not have to handle every individual item themselves.

We work at whatever pace the situation requires. Some families want it done as quickly as possible. Others need more time to review items before they are removed. We accommodate both and communicate throughout the process.

Discreet and Respectful

We understand that hoarding is a sensitive subject. Our crew arrives without signage that draws attention, works professionally, and treats the home and its contents with respect. Everything discussed about the job stays between us and the client. We are here to help, not to judge.

Whether you are a family member, a caregiver, a social worker, or a property owner, we work with you to make the process as manageable as possible.
