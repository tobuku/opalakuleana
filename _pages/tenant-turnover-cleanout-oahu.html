The Problem With Tenant Turnovers

When a tenant moves out of a rental on Oahu, what they leave behind can range from a few bags of trash to an entire apartment's worth of furniture, clothing, and household items. For property managers and landlords, every day a unit sits full of someone else's belongings is a day it is not generating rent.

The City's bulky pickup program is not built for this scenario — the wait times do not match your leasing schedule. Handling it yourself means renting a truck, hauling loads to the transfer station, and spending hours on labor that pulls you away from finding your next tenant. And for multi-unit buildings, the problem multiplies.

How We Handle Tenant Turnovers

We come to the property, walk every room, and assess everything that needs to go. We give you a firm quote for the entire cleanout. Once approved, we clear the unit — furniture, appliances, trash, personal belongings, whatever was left behind.

Our crew handles all lifting, loading, and hauling. We sweep the cleared areas and leave the space ready for your maintenance or renovation team. If the tenant left behind a full kitchen, bedroom sets, a packed garage, or all of the above, we take care of it in one coordinated effort.

We work around your schedule. If you need the unit cleared before a specific date — a new tenant's move-in, a contractor's start date, or an inspection — we plan around that timeline.
