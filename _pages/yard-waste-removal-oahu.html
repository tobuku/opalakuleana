Oahu Yards Never Stop Growing

Hawaii's tropical climate does not give your yard a break. Trees, hedges, and ground cover grow year-round, and after any trimming session — or after a passing storm — you can be left with piles of green waste that dwarf what a standard trash can can handle.

The City and County of Honolulu does offer green waste collection, but it comes with real limitations: material must fit inside your green bin, branches cannot exceed three feet in length, and palm fronds that still have a trunk attached are typically rejected. If an arborist spent the morning dropping a large monkeypod or trimming a row of hedges, you may be looking at a pile that would take weeks to bin-and-bag your way through.

Opala Kuleana hauls yard waste across Oahu. We load everything — palm fronds, branches, stumps, trimmings, soil, and debris — and take it to a permitted facility. One call, one trip, yard cleared.

When This Is Not the Right Option

If you have one or two bags of grass clippings or a small pile of trimmings that fit inside your green bin, use your City curbside collection — that is exactly what it is there for and it costs you nothing.

If you need trees or hedges actually cut down, we are not the right call at that step. Hire a licensed arborist or tree service to do the cutting. Once the debris is on the ground, that is where we come in — we haul out everything they leave behind.

Where we make the most sense: the volume is too large for your green bin, the City rejected your pile for exceeding branch length limits, or you want the yard cleared on your schedule rather than waiting for the next collection date.

Why Yard Waste Is a Bigger Problem on Oahu

Wet, heavy palm fronds are awkward to move and impossible to compact. A single large queen palm can leave fifty to a hundred fronds on the ground, and each one can weigh several pounds when freshly cut. Coconut trunks and large monkeypod limbs are denser still. Filling a truck with palm fronds takes volume fast; filling it with soil or gravel takes weight fast. Both have their own disposal challenges.

The City's green waste program routes combustible material to H-POWER, Oahu's waste-to-energy facility on Sand Island. For larger loads, permitted transfer stations like PVT Land Company accept green waste and inert debris. Getting material to those facilities requires a truck and a valid load — which is the part most homeowners do not have covered.

We handle the transport and drop-off. All disposal is permitted. No illegal dumping.

Pricing

Yard waste pricing depends on what you have:

  • Green waste (fronds, branches, trimmings): priced by truck volume. Palm fronds and branches are bulky but light — they fill a truck fast, so the quote reflects the space used.
  • Soil, gravel, and rock: priced by weight. Heavy material costs more to transport and dispose of. If you have a mix of green waste and dirt, we will quote each component separately if the volume is significant.
  • Mixed loads: one trip, one quote — we factor in both the volume and the weight of what we are hauling.

We give you a firm price before any work starts. No surprise charges when the truck is full.
