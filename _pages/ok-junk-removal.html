Saw Our Truck?

If you spotted a truck on the H-1, H-2, or anywhere across Oahu with "OK JUNK REMOVAL 536-6075" on the side, that's us. OK Junk Removal is the on-the-road name for ʻŌpala Kuleana — a Hawaiian-owned junk removal service based on Oahu and operating island-wide since 2005.

The "OK" comes from our full name: ʻŌpala Kuleana. We put it on the trucks so it's easy to read at highway speed. Now that you've found us, here's what we do.

What We Haul

We remove just about anything that isn't hazardous waste:

  • Furniture — couches, recliners, dining sets, dressers, desks
  • Appliances — fridges, washers, dryers, stoves, water heaters
  • E-waste & electronics — TVs, computers, monitors, printers
  • Construction debris — lumber, drywall, tile, concrete, scrap metal
  • Estate cleanouts — full-property clearing for families and realtors
  • Yard waste — palm fronds, branches, stumps, green debris
  • Mattresses, hot tubs, storage units, commercial spaces

How It Works

  1. Call or book online — reach us at 808-536-6075 or request a free estimate.
  2. Free on-site estimate — we come to you, look at the job, and give you a firm price on the spot.
  3. We haul it away — once you approve, we do all the lifting, loading, and disposal. We sweep up before we leave.

No hidden fees. No hourly billing. You only pay for the truck space your items use, and the price we quote is the price you pay.
